How the number is built. MyRating = FPI + TalentTilt + PortalAdj + RetProdAdj. Talent pulls FPI toward a tier-specific talent-implied rating (separate Power-4 and G5 fits). Portal adds points per On3 net-movement unit. Returning production adds points for production above the national average, plus a QB flag (returning / transfer / new). Each rating carries a ± band that widens when signals are missing or conflict. The weighting toggle rescales all tilts. Spread calculator uses venue-specific HFA (altitude & crowd), anchored near 2.5. Preseason tilts decay with the calendar: held flat through wk 2 then faded to a floor, a schedule measured by replaying 2,262 games of point-in-time FPI (backtest.py). FPI itself refreshes weekly, so late-season lines lean on it. Not yet modeled: coaching changes, injuries, schedule strength; weights are sensitivity-tested, not backtested.
For research and entertainment only — not betting or financial advice. Markets are efficient; no model beats them reliably — and this one has been measured, not assumed, to fall short of the closing line. It is a tool for finding disagreements worth understanding.

#TeamFPIMy Rating Edge vs MktWin TotRegQBRoster
How the number is built. MyRating = FPI + regime × (RegTilt + QBAdj + RosterAdj). RegTilt = −(0.06 × 2025 turnover margin + 0.40 × close-game luck), capped ±2.5 — fades teams whose record/margin was luck-inflated, buys the unlucky. QBAdj: star QB back from injury +0.6 to +1.3, clear new-QB downgrade −1.2 to −1.5, unsettled battle 0 (wider band). Roster: ±0.5–0.8. Market rating is implied from 2026 win totals; "Edge vs Mkt" is MyRating minus that. Tilts are deliberately small because NFL FPI and the market are both sharp. Injuries: a player ESPN lists Out or Doubtful moves that week's margin only — by his value on the hand-kept list, or 3.0 for a starting QB not on it. Injured Reserve is left to FPI, which has already rated the games without him. NFL HFA ~1.8 and noisy. Preseason tilts are held flat through wk 9 then faded — the NFL window measured over 816 games in backtest.py. FPI itself refreshes weekly.
For research and entertainment only — not betting or financial advice. NFL markets are highly efficient; no model beats them reliably.
