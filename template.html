Portal adds points per On3 net-movement unit. Returning production adds points for production above the national average, plus a QB flag (returning / transfer / new). Spread calculator uses venue-specific HFA (altitude & crowd), anchored near 2.5. For research and entertainment only — not betting or financial advice. Markets are efficient; no model beats them reliably — and this one has been measured, not assumed, to fall short of the closing line. It is a tool for finding disagreements worth understanding.

How the number is built. MyRating = FPI + regime × (RegTilt + QBAdj + RosterAdj). RegTilt = −(0.06 × 2025 turnover margin + 0.40 × close-game luck), capped ±2.5 — fades teams whose record/margin was luck-inflated, buys the unlucky. QBAdj: star QB back from injury +0.6 to +1.3, clear new-QB downgrade −1.2 to −1.5, unsettled battle 0 (wider band). Roster: ±0.5–0.8. Market rating is implied from 2026 win totals; "Edge vs Mkt" is MyRating minus that. Tilts are deliberately small because NFL FPI and the market are both sharp. NFL HFA ~1.8 and noisy. Preseason tilts are held flat through wk 9 then faded — the NFL window measured over 816 games in backtest.py. FPI itself refreshes weekly.
